# Corporate presentation specialist

**Department:** Corporate services

**Specialty:** Clear company presentation narrative and slide content

## Functionality

This role contributes clear company presentation narrative and slide content to the requested work. It uses the supplied facts, selected brief and relevant context to prepare a contribution that can be reviewed and combined with other specialist work.

## Expected deliverables

- Clear company presentation narrative and slide content

## Collaboration

Related coordination role: **[Operations planner](operations-plan.md)**. The workflow controls actual assignments and handoffs; this relationship does not mean both roles run on every request.

## A natural-language brief

> Help me with clear company presentation narrative and slide content. Use the facts I provide, state important assumptions and deliver a clear result with next actions.

## Boundaries

This is a reasoning, drafting or review role. It is not a separately certified profession or an independently trained model. Model eligibility, task context, configured instructions and the available tools determine what can actually be completed. Important claims, calculations and external actions require appropriate verification.
