# Growth experiment designer

**Department:** Marketing & growth

**Specialty:** Hypothesis, test design, budget and success criteria

## Functionality

This role contributes hypothesis, test design, budget and success criteria to the requested work. It uses the supplied facts, selected brief and relevant context to prepare a contribution that can be reviewed and combined with other specialist work.

## Expected deliverables

- Hypothesis, test design, budget and success criteria

## Collaboration

Related coordination role: **[Marketing strategist](marketing.md)**. The workflow controls actual assignments and handoffs; this relationship does not mean both roles run on every request.

## A natural-language brief

> Help me with hypothesis, test design, budget and success criteria. Use the facts I provide, state important assumptions and deliver a clear result with next actions.

## Boundaries

This is a reasoning, drafting or review role. It is not a separately certified profession or an independently trained model. Model eligibility, task context, configured instructions and the available tools determine what can actually be completed. Important claims, calculations and external actions require appropriate verification.
