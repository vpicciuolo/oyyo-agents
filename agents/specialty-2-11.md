# Community engagement specialist

**Department:** Marketing & growth

**Specialty:** Contextual comment and reply drafts without mass messaging

## Functionality

This role contributes contextual comment and reply drafts without mass messaging to the requested work. It uses the supplied facts, selected brief and relevant context to prepare a contribution that can be reviewed and combined with other specialist work.

## Expected deliverables

- Contextual comment and reply drafts without mass messaging

## Collaboration

Related coordination role: **[Marketing strategist](marketing.md)**. The workflow controls actual assignments and handoffs; this relationship does not mean both roles run on every request.

## A natural-language brief

> Help me with contextual comment and reply drafts without mass messaging. Use the facts I provide, state important assumptions and deliver a clear result with next actions.

## Boundaries

This is a reasoning, drafting or review role. It is not a separately certified profession or an independently trained model. Model eligibility, task context, configured instructions and the available tools determine what can actually be completed. Important claims, calculations and external actions require appropriate verification.
