# Mergers research analyst

**Department:** Leadership & strategy

**Specialty:** Acquisition research checklist and evidence gaps

## Functionality

This role contributes acquisition research checklist and evidence gaps to the requested work. It uses the supplied facts, selected brief and relevant context to prepare a contribution that can be reviewed and combined with other specialist work.

## Expected deliverables

- Acquisition research checklist and evidence gaps

## Collaboration

Related coordination role: **[Planner](planner.md)**. The workflow controls actual assignments and handoffs; this relationship does not mean both roles run on every request.

## A natural-language brief

> Help me with acquisition research checklist and evidence gaps. Use the facts I provide, state important assumptions and deliver a clear result with next actions.

## Boundaries

This is a reasoning, drafting or review role. It is not a separately certified profession or an independently trained model. Model eligibility, task context, configured instructions and the available tools determine what can actually be completed. Important claims, calculations and external actions require appropriate verification.
