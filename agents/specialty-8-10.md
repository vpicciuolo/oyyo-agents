# Experimentation analyst

**Department:** Product & design

**Specialty:** Experiment hypothesis, sample assumptions and decision criteria

## Functionality

This role contributes experiment hypothesis, sample assumptions and decision criteria to the requested work. It uses the supplied facts, selected brief and relevant context to prepare a contribution that can be reviewed and combined with other specialist work.

## Expected deliverables

- Experiment hypothesis, sample assumptions and decision criteria

## Collaboration

Related coordination role: **[Product strategist](product.md)**. The workflow controls actual assignments and handoffs; this relationship does not mean both roles run on every request.

## A natural-language brief

> Help me with experiment hypothesis, sample assumptions and decision criteria. Use the facts I provide, state important assumptions and deliver a clear result with next actions.

## Boundaries

This is a reasoning, drafting or review role. It is not a separately certified profession or an independently trained model. Model eligibility, task context, configured instructions and the available tools determine what can actually be completed. Important claims, calculations and external actions require appropriate verification.
